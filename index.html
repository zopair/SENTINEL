Research question

Redirect exploration. Measure the result.

SENTINEL investigates whether controlled decoy environments can steer scripted agent exploration toward fictional assets while real systems remain outside the maze. This is an experimental hypothesis—not a promise of permanent deception or guaranteed defense.

Capabilities

Focused MVP. Ready for controlled evaluation.

⌘

Adaptive synthetic labyrinth

Procedural decoy graph with explicit limits for nodes, depth, steps, and challenge behavior.

▤

Fictional decoy assets

Coherent cross-referenced documents; no real credentials, personal data, or production secrets.

◎

Agent behavior simulation

Six scripted synthetic profiles for repeatable controlled exploration.

⇄

Policy benchmarking

Baseline FIFO vs adaptive priority using matched seeds, budgets, and JSONL telemetry.

⬡

Containment test fixtures

Rootless Podman CI checks for network/filesystem restrictions and resource boundaries.

⌁

Reproducible telemetry

Deterministic seeds, explicit budgets, operator provenance, and experiment outputs.

Scope & safety

Research prototype—not a production honeypot

Do not connect SENTINEL to production networks or expose it publicly. External untrusted agents remain disabled pending independent containment review. CI checks are not proof of escape-proof isolation or certification.
